## 3.1 About LAMIKAA Naturals

LAMIKAA Naturals is a farmer-owned beauty brand of Bokakhat Agro Organic Producer Co. Ltd. (BAOPCL), a Farmer Producer Company (FPC) established to create greater value and better opportunities for farmers.

Rooted in the indigenous knowledge and rich natural heritage of Assam and Northeast India, LAMIKAA Naturals combines traditional wisdom with modern cosmetic science to create conscious, nature-inspired beauty products.

But LAMIKAA is more than a beauty brand. It is a farmer-owned enterprise where the success of the brand is connected directly to the prosperity of its farmer members.

LAMIKAA Naturals is owned by Bokakhat Agro Organic Producer Co. Ltd., an FPC owned by its farmer members. Therefore, the value created through LAMIKAA contributes to the farmer-owned enterprise, and profits distributed by BAOPCL can reach its member farmers as dividends, subject to applicable laws and the company's dividend declaration.

When LAMIKAA grows, our farmers grow with us.

Every product carries a bigger purpose — to create value from nature, strengthen the farmer-owned value chain and contribute to a better quality of life for farming families.

## 3.2 Brand philosophy — Indigenous Wisdom. Modern Science. Responsible Beauty.

LAMIKAA Naturals believes that the future of beauty can be inspired by the wisdom of the past. For generations, communities across Assam and Northeast India have developed knowledge of plants, herbs and natural resources and their traditional uses. LAMIKAA seeks to respect this heritage and bring it into the modern world through contemporary cosmetic science, responsible sourcing and quality-focused product development.

Our philosophy is built on four pillars:

- **Indigenous Knowledge** — Respecting the traditional wisdom and natural heritage of our region.
- **Modern Cosmetic Science** — Combining traditional ingredients and knowledge with modern formulation, research and quality standards.
- **Farmer Ownership** — Creating a business where farmers participate not only in supplying raw materials but also in the economic value created by the enterprise.
- **Responsible Beauty** — Building products and processes that respect people, communities and the environment.

LAMIKAA is where the wisdom of nature meets the science of modern beauty — and where beauty creates value for farmers.

## 3.3 Brand motive — Beauty That Creates Prosperity for Farmers

The fundamental motive behind LAMIKAA Naturals is simple: to create more value for the farmers who produce the natural resources behind our products.

In a conventional agricultural value chain, farmers often sell raw produce at commodity prices while greater value is created later through processing, branding and marketing. LAMIKAA Naturals seeks to change this equation. As a brand owned by Bokakhat Agro Organic Producer Co. Ltd., a Farmer Producer Company, LAMIKAA forms part of a farmer-owned value chain.

The journey is: **Farmer → FPC → Value Addition → LAMIKAA Naturals → Consumer → Profit → Farmer Members**

When the business succeeds and profits are distributed by BAOPCL, its farmer members can benefit through dividends. This creates an important connection between consumer purchase and farmer prosperity. Your choice of LAMIKAA can help create value beyond the product — value that can ultimately return to the farming community.

Our long-term ambition is to help farmers earn more from what they grow, participate in value addition and gradually improve their family's standard of living.

## 3.4 Brand impact — Financial: From Raw Produce to Shared Value

LAMIKAA Naturals aims to help farmers move from being producers of raw materials to becoming participants in a higher-value business ecosystem. Instead of value ending at the farm gate, processing, branding and marketing create additional economic value. Because LAMIKAA Naturals is owned by BAOPCL, a Farmer Producer Company, the success of the brand contributes to the farmer-owned enterprise. Profits generated by the business, when declared for distribution in accordance with applicable laws and company decisions, can reach the member farmers through dividends.

Our financial impact: creates opportunities for greater value realization from agricultural produce; enables farmers to participate in value addition; strengthens the farmer-owned business ecosystem; creates opportunities for higher farmer earnings; allows farmers to benefit from the profitability of their own enterprise; helps contribute to improved household income and living standards.

Our goal is not simply to sell more products. Our goal is to create more value — and share that value with the farmers who own the enterprise.

## 3.5 Brand impact — Social: When Farmers Prosper, Families Prosper

The impact of LAMIKAA extends beyond income. Higher and more stable economic opportunities can help farmer families invest in the things that matter most — education, healthcare, housing, children's future and family wellbeing. LAMIKAA Naturals therefore aims to create a business model where commercial success can contribute to social development in farming communities. Through the farmer-owned structure of BAOPCL, the growth of LAMIKAA can contribute to strengthening the economic foundation of member farmers and their families.

Our social vision includes: supporting better educational opportunities for farmer children; improving access to healthcare and family wellbeing; helping families become more financially secure; creating dignity and confidence among farming communities; encouraging younger generations to see agriculture and agribusiness as viable opportunities; creating rural employment and entrepreneurship opportunities through value addition.

A successful brand should not only create customers — it should create better opportunities for the people who make the brand possible.

## 3.6 Brand impact — Environmental: Beauty That Gives Back to Nature

LAMIKAA Naturals believes that the future of beauty must be connected to the future of agriculture and the environment. Our connection with farmers gives us an opportunity to encourage organic and sustainable farming practices and responsible use of natural resources. By creating value from agricultural and natural resources through responsible processing and value addition, we aim to build a business model that connects healthy farms, healthy ecosystems and responsible beauty.

Our environmental priorities include: supporting organic and sustainable agriculture; encouraging responsible management of natural resources; promoting healthier agro-ecosystems; supporting biodiversity-friendly farming approaches; creating greater value from agricultural resources through value addition; building a responsible farm-to-beauty value chain.

We believe beauty should enhance our relationship with nature — not compromise it.

## 3.7 The LAMIKAA Difference — A Beauty Brand Owned by Farmers

LAMIKAA Naturals is different because behind the brand is not simply a corporate ownership structure. Behind LAMIKAA are farmers.

**LAMIKAA Naturals → Owned by Bokakhat Agro Organic Producer Co. Ltd. → A Farmer Producer Company → Owned by Farmer Members → Profits can return to members through declared dividends → Greater opportunity for farmer income and improved quality of life**

This is the heart of our story. When you choose LAMIKAA, you are choosing beauty with a purpose. You are supporting a farmer-owned enterprise that seeks to transform natural resources into higher-value products, create economic opportunities and build a more prosperous future for farming families.

## 3.9 Copy rules (apply to every prompt that writes copy)

1. **Brand name casing:** "LAMIKAA NATURALS" in wordmarks/uppercase contexts, "LAMIKAA Naturals" in running text, never "Lamika" or "Lamikaa Natural". Company: "Bokakhat Agro Organic Producer Co. Ltd. (BAOPCL)".
2. **Preserve the legal qualifiers** whenever profits or dividends are mentioned: profits "can" / "may" reach member farmers "through dividends", "subject to applicable laws and the company's dividend declaration". Never promise a guaranteed payout, a percentage, or a figure.
3. **Cosmetic claims only.** Describe cosmetic benefits (cleanses, hydrates, brightens the look of, softens, refreshes, helps skin appear even and radiant). Never use medical language (treats, cures, heals, anti-acne, removes pigmentation, dermatologist-tested, clinically proven) unless the owner supplies proof later; leave such statements as placeholders.
4. **Trust badges** ("Farmer to Consumer", "100% Organic", "Result Oriented") are required by the owner on product cards. Implement them as a configurable array in the brand config module (Section 8.1) — never hard-code them in components — so the owner can adjust wording for compliance.
5. **Ingredient facts:** Black rice is the hero ingredient across the range. Keep ingredient claims generic and widely accepted (e.g. "rich in antioxidants", "traditionally valued in Northeast India"). Do not name a specific rice variety, region of cultivation, or certification unless legible on the packaging.
6. **No fabricated social proof.** Do not invent named customers, testimonials, press quotes, ratings counts or awards. If the codebase has a reviews feature, preserve it; any seeded reviews must be flagged `isSample: true` and hidden by default behind a brand-config flag (Section 8.3).
7. **Tone:** warm, confident, sensorial, unhurried; short sentences; second person; emotion before specification; never salesy caps-lock urgency.
